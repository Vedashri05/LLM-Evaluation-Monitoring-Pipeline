NimbusDesk offers three plans. The Starter plan costs $12 per user per month and includes 5 GB of storage. The Team plan costs $29 per user per month and includes 100 GB of storage and priority email support. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and includes unlimited storage, single sign-on, and a dedicated account manager.

All plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start the trial.
